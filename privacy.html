Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how ALL-POST handles information when you use the ALL-POST website and social publishing features. ALL-POST is a single-owner workspace operated by Alex Getahun. It is not a multi-user social publishing service.

Information handled by ALL-POST

  • Workspace access: the password you enter is checked by the service to sign you in. The app uses a signed, secure, HTTP-only session cookie; it does not store the password in your browser.
  • Social account connections: if you connect LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube, ALL-POST receives account identifiers and profile details returned by that provider, plus OAuth access and refresh tokens where provided. Tokens are encrypted by the app before they are stored in private Vercel Blob storage.
  • Post content: the text and media you select are sent to each selected social platform when you publish. TikTok uploads require you to confirm consent and select publishing settings. ALL-POST does not currently keep a server-side post history or schedule posts.
  • Uploaded media: photos and videos you add are stored in a public Vercel Blob store so social platforms can retrieve them. Anyone who has an uploaded media URL can access that file. ALL-POST does not automatically remove media after publishing; it remains stored until you remove it from the composer or request deletion.
  • Drafts: the composer may save a draft in your browser's local storage on that device. Clearing the browser's site data removes that local draft.
  • Technical data: Vercel, which hosts the website and API, may process request and security data such as IP address, device/browser details, timestamps, and error information as part of operating and protecting the service.

How information is used

Information is used to secure workspace access, connect your selected accounts, display connected profiles, publish only when you request it, refresh provider connections, transfer media to selected platforms, and operate and protect the service. The app does not currently sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Where information is processed

Account information and posts are processed by the social platforms you connect under their own terms and privacy policies. ALL-POST stores OAuth connections in private Vercel Blob storage, encrypted by the application before storage. Media is held in public Vercel Blob storage for retrieval by the platforms. Vercel provides hosting and storage infrastructure and may process technical request data. The app also loads fonts from Google Fonts when the main workspace is opened, which may send technical request information to Google. These providers handle information under their own terms and policies.

Retention and deletion

Connected account tokens remain stored while the connection is in use. You can request removal of stored account data by emailing the address below and can revoke ALL-POST’s authorization in the relevant social platform’s app settings. Uploaded media remains publicly reachable by its URL until you remove it from the composer or request deletion. Drafts remain in this browser until you clear them or remove the site's local storage. Vercel may retain operational logs according to its own retention practices.

Security

ALL-POST uses HTTPS on its production site, secure session cookies, and application encryption for stored social account tokens. Public media URLs are accessible to anyone with the link. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your workspace password private and contact us promptly if you believe the workspace or a connected account was accessed without permission.

Your choices and privacy requests

You choose whether to connect social accounts, authorize publishing, upload media, and submit each post. You can revoke app access in each platform, remove uploaded media from the composer, clear local drafts from your browser, or ask about access, correction, or deletion of information handled by ALL-POST. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under local law.

For privacy questions or requests, email alexgetahuntadese@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials in your message.

Children

ALL-POST is not designed for children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13, or the minimum age required to use the connected services in their location.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
